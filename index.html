FAQ

Common Questions

To help you better understand how our platform works, here are answers to some common questions.

Is this a freelance bidding platform like Upwork?+

No. This is not a bidding marketplace. We review applicants internally and contact suitable individuals for relevant assignments when available.

How will I receive instructions?+

Assignment details and coordination are typically shared through WhatsApp or email, depending on the nature of the work.

Are there any joining fees or paid memberships?+

No. We do not charge applicants to apply, join, or be considered for assignments.

Is there a minimum time commitment?+

There is no fixed minimum commitment. Assignment availability depends on business needs and profile suitability.

How do you assess whether I am suitable?+

We review your submitted details and may request a short screening task or skills check where relevant.
